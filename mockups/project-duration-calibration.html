Phase 1 (revised) — Project Duration Calibration

Pivoted from per-task hours (no estimate field exists) to per-project duration (planned weeks vs actual weeks). New section on the Insights tab. Read-only, retrospective. All numbers below are mock.

Where it slots: Below the existing "💡 Project Insights" KPI strip, above the by-Size chart. The existing by-Size table absorbs Section B and adds the multiplier + recommendation columns. Sections A and C are new.
Design question this mockup tries to resolve: Should "planned" mean the form's end_date (original commitment) or working_due (latest forecast)? The toggle at the top of the Calibration By Category section lets the user pick. Both are valuable: end_date answers "did we hit our promise"; working_due answers "did we get better at forecasting as we went." Default is end_date since that's what partner departments remember.
