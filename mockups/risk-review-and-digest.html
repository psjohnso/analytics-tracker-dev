Risk badge on Project Review & the Needs-Attention digest

Two surfaces that put the in-flight risk score where decisions happen. Illustrative data, consistent with the risk score already built.

Surface 1

Risk badge on Project Review

A 🟢🟡🔴 badge on each project in the weekly review, with a "Why?" expander that drops the per-factor breakdown right next to the review notes — so the conversation starts from the risk drivers. Leads & admins only.

Where it slots: the Project Review tab, inline on each project row. The breakdown shares the expanded row with the meeting notes / decisions / action items, so risk informs the discussion in place.
