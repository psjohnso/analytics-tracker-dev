Duration estimate on the project form

A reference-class estimate that appears as the user fills out a new project — driven by the two variables that actually predict duration in your data: category and partner department. Live and interactive: change the dropdowns below to see it update. Medians are your real numbers from the analysis.

Where it slots: in the project form's Timeline section, just under the Target end date field. It's an aid, never a lock — one click fills the suggested date, or the user ignores it. Allocation/effort isn't used (your data showed it isn't recorded reliably enough yet).
