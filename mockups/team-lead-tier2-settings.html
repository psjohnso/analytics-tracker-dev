Team Lead — Tier 2 settings (per-team config)

Mockups of the three Tier 2 editors a Team Lead would get, scoped to their own team. Built on the same per-team-config pattern: a config keyed by team, an accessor that reads the current team's slice, and a save that merges only that team's entry. Shown here from the perspective of the Architects lead.

Viewing as Jordan Reyes · Architects Lead (not an admin)
How permission works. A Team Lead sees a Settings nav group named after their team, holding only what they may edit for it. They are locked to their own team (no team picker). An admin sees the same editors plus a team dropdown to switch between teams. Every save merges only the current team's slice, so a lead can never overwrite another team's config.
