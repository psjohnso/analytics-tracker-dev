Team Load Analytics

Where the team's hours actually went over the last 12 months. Uses only completed-project time entries — no estimates required. New section on the Insights tab. Unit filter at the top scopes all four pieces (KPIs + Sections A, B, C) — pick a unit to see that unit's slice of the picture.

Where it slots: Below the existing Team Donut + Completion Timeline row. The donut already shows total hours per team member; this section adds the structural context (partner workload, lead share, concentration).
Visibility: Section C (per-person concentration) shows by name only to team leads and to each person's own row, matching the pattern from Duration Calibration. Sections A and B aggregate across the team and are visible to everyone.

Questions this section helps answer:
  • "Are we over-invested in any one partner department?" — Section A. Surfaces the "Water is 17% of our year" point that's worth knowing during planning conversations with the CIO.
  • "Which categories have lone specialists carrying the load?" — Section B's lead-share + the "Read" column flag where the lead is the only person who knows the work. Geospatial at 80% is a succession-planning signal.
  • "Who's spread too thin? Who has concentration risk?" — Section C. Both directions matter — Member A at 7 projects with no clear focus may benefit from consolidation; Member D at 82% on one project is a single-thread risk if that project blocks.
  • "What's the team-load shape we should reproduce next year, and what's the shape we should avoid?" — All three together give the retro narrative.
