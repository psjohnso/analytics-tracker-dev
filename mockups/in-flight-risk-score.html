In-Flight Project Risk Score

A weekly 🟢🟡🔴 early-warning score for active projects, built from leading indicators you already track. Transparent and additive — every score shows exactly which factors drove it, so it's a conversation starter, not a black box. Numbers are illustrative.

Where it surfaces: primary home is the Project Review tab (a badge per project + expandable drivers — decisions happen there weekly). Plus an "At Risk" rollup on Insights/Overview, and a small risk chip on project cards and a lead's My Work.
The model

How the score is built

Additive, weighted, and degrades gracefully: each factor contributes 0 → its max based on a 0–1 severity from concrete thresholds. Score = Σ(points) normalized to 0–100 over the factors that have data. A missing factor (e.g. no snapshot history yet) is dropped and its weight redistributed — projects are never penalized for missing data.

All inputs come from existing data: project start_date/end_date/working_due, the calibrated expected duration (Duration Calibration), tasks (status/due_date/actual_end/CreationDate), allocations + snapshots, and assignee mix. Weights/thresholds live in app_config so you can tune without code.

Design choices worth confirming:
  • Transparent & tunable — additive points, drivers always shown, weights/thresholds in app_config. No ML, no black box (right call at your data volume).
  • Graceful degradation — the snapshot-dependent factor is excluded until history accrues, so this ships useful today and gets sharper over time.
  • Scope — active/scheduled projects only; recomputed client-side on each load.
  • Open question: default weights above are a starting point — we'd calibrate them against a few projects you already know were "troubled" to make sure the score agrees with your gut.
