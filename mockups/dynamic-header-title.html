Dynamic header title & subtitle by team

The header starts generic, then resolves to the signed-in user's team. Recommended style: team name as the title, a steady descriptor as the subtitle.

Recommended (Style A below): title = the user's team; subtitle = "Project & Task Tracker · City of Tucson IT" (or the team's configured eyebrow). Falls back to a generic "Project Tracker" before login or when a user has no team.

Where the text comes from

  • Title = the signed-in user's team (CURRENT_TEAM / their owning_team); admins follow the team switcher ("All Teams" when unscoped). Before login → generic "Project Tracker."
  • Subtitle = a steady descriptor ("Project & Task Tracker · City of Tucson IT"), or — if you'd like it fully per-team — the team's configured team_intro eyebrow, falling back to the generic line.
  • Updates live on login and on admin team/role switch (same hook as the header stats). Version badge unchanged.
